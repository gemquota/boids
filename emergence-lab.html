One continuous material history. Protons, neutrons and electrons begin independently distributed. Nuclear binding creates primordial nuclei; recombination creates atoms; gravity gathers gas into collapsing clouds and stars. Stars synthesise heavier elements, slowly shed enriched winds, then follow mass-dependent evolutionary paths. Supernovae launch spatially explicit ejecta clouds; remnants continue gravitating. This is an accelerated, 2D educational model, not a calibrated cosmological or stellar-structure solver.
