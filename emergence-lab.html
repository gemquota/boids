01 · Atoms: persistent elemental particles with mass, radius, charge, valence, momentum and thermal motion. 02 · Molecules: discovered automatically as connected components of actual atom bonds. Formula, composition, topology and centre of mass are derived from their constituent atoms. 03 · Molecular assemblies: nearby molecules experience weak non-covalent attraction and form transient spatial assemblies without consuming atomic valence or inventing covalent bonds. This is a coarse-grained educational model, not calibrated quantum chemistry.
