Particles retain their own identities. The highlighted cargo particles form a loose resource cloud that follows a moving cell anchor. Two moving cells each carry their own loose material cloud. Collect nearby to acquire resources, Inventory to inspect a stockpile, Craft C-H molecule to bond atoms, Harvest energy to draw from a finite light reservoir, Metabolise CH to release chemical energy, and Share resources to diffuse materials between nearby cells. Collisions exchange momentum; bonds are elastic, exert forces and can break. The display is a deliberately simplified 2D projection of the worker's 3D-capable particle state.
