This is a conceptual, accelerated 2D model. Particle encounters, nuclear assembly, gravity and stellar evolution are simplified to make transformations visible. The quantities are model state, not precision measurements of the real universe. The current lab demonstrates the cosmological opening of a much longer emergence journey; molecular and cellular stages are separate integration work, not secretly happening offscreen.
